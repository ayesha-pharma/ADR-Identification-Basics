# ADR Case Analysis

## Case 1 – Methotrexate and Mouth Ulcers

### Patient Information
- Age: 52 years
- Sex: Female
- Indication: Rheumatoid arthritis

### Medication History
- Methotrexate: 10 mg once weekly
- Folic acid: Concomitant medication

### Adverse Event
Mouth ulcers

### Timeline
Methotrexate initiated → Mouth ulcers developed after treatment initiation.

### Assessment

| Parameter | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Suspected drug | Methotrexate |
| Adverse event | Mouth ulcers |
| Temporal relationship | Plausible |
| Dechallenge | Not reported |
| Rechallenge | Not reported |
| Alternative causes | To be assessed |
| ADR assessment | Potential ADR |

### Rationale

Methotrexate was identified as the suspected drug based on the reported temporal relationship and the known association between methotrexate therapy and oral mucosal adverse effects.
