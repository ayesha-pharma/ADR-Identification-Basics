# Individual Case Safety Report (ICSR)

## Project Overview

This simulated pharmacovigilance project demonstrates the end-to-end assessment of an Individual Case Safety Report (ICSR), including adverse drug reaction identification, case validity, seriousness assessment, expectedness assessment, causality assessment, and case documentation.

The case simulates the role of a Drug Safety Associate processing a spontaneous adverse event report received from a healthcare professional.

# Case Narrative

A spontaneous report was received from a healthcare professional concerning a 45-year-old female patient receiving metformin 500 mg twice daily for the treatment of type 2 diabetes mellitus.

Five days after initiation of metformin therapy, the patient developed nausea and diarrhea. The symptoms affected the patient's daily activities but did not require hospitalization.

The physician reduced the metformin dose, following which the patient's symptoms improved and subsequently resolved.

No rechallenge was performed.

No significant alternative cause was reported in the available case information.

# 11. Pharmacovigilance Case Processing Workflow

The case was assessed using the following workflow:

1. Confirm minimum ICSR validity criteria
2. Identify the suspected medicinal product
3. Identify and characterize the adverse event
4. Review the temporal relationship
5. Assess seriousness
6. Assess severity
7. Assess expectedness against the applicable safety reference
8. Evaluate dechallenge and rechallenge information
9. Assess causality
10. Document the case narrative and assessment
